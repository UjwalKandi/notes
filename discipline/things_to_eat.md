## 1.2 — BMR Calculation

### BMR — Mifflin-St Jeor Formula

```
BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5
    = (10 × 62.6) + (6.25 × 170) − (5 × 28) + 5
    = 626 + 1,062.5 − 140 + 5

BMR = 1,554 kcal/day
```

> This is the minimum energy the body burns at complete rest — just to breathe, circulate blood, and maintain organ function.

Since the goal is body recomposition — simultaneously losing fat AND building muscle — a moderate deficit is ideal. Aggressive cuts destroy muscle, and a bulk adds unwanted fat to an already lean-fat frame.

| Phase | TDEE | Deficit | Daily Target |

> A 300–400 kcal daily deficit = approximately 0.6–0.8 lbs of fat loss per week while preserving and building muscle. Combined with high protein and progressive overload, this is the sweet spot for recomposition on a lean-fat frame.
