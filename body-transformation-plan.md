## SECTION 5 — Supplement Stack: Timing, Dosage & Absorption Protocol

### Design Philosophy

> **1. Mineral Competition Separation** — Zinc and Magnesium share the same intestinal transport pathway. Taking them together reduces absorption of both by up to 40%. The Mon/Wed/Fri = Magnesium and Tue/Thu/Sat = Zinc split directly solves this.

> **2. Adaptogen Cycling** — Ashwagandha and HGW/Maca are most effective when NOT taken daily. Daily use leads to receptor downregulation within 2–4 weeks. The 3-days-on alternating pattern prevents this and maintains long-term sensitivity.

> **3. Fat-Soluble Supplement Rotation** — Alternating Multivitamin and Fish Oil across days avoids accumulating fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) beyond safe thresholds, while ensuring both are consistently absorbed with dietary fat across the week.

---

### Standing Daily Rule — Probiotic (Not in Table)

**Take every single morning, first thing, on an empty stomach, 15–20 minutes before food and before any other supplement.**

Stomach acid is at its lowest before eating. Live probiotic cultures survive transit most effectively in this window and colonize the gut for improved nutrient absorption across all meals. Dose: per label (typically 1–2 capsules).

### Supplement Dosage & Placement Reference

| Supplement | Dose | Timing Slot | Why This Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Probiotic** | Per label (1–2 capsules) | First thing AM, empty stomach, daily | Lowest stomach acid before eating — live cultures survive transit and colonize gut most effectively |
| **Multivitamin & Minerals** | 1 tablet | Morning with fat-containing meal | Fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K require dietary fat for absorption. Egg sandwich or oatmeal provides this. |

| **Creatine Monohydrate** | 5g | Post-workout daily; morning on rest/washout days | Daily dosing maintains muscle creatine saturation. Insulin spike from oatmeal carbs drives creatine into muscle cells more efficiently. |
| **Magnesium Glycinate** | 300–400mg | Bedtime, Mon/Wed/Fri | Glycinate form is most bioavailable. Taken at night: improves deep sleep, reduces muscle cramps, supports testosterone, manages overnight cortisol. Separated from Zinc nights entirely. |
| **Ashwagandha** | 300–600mg | Bedtime, Mon/Wed/Fri (same as Magnesium) | Reduces evening cortisol (the primary fat-storage hormone for lean-fat physiques), promotes deeper sleep, supports testosterone recovery. Greatest cortisol reduction when taken close to sleep. |

---

### Sunday System Washout — Rationale

Taking a 24-hour supplement break once per week achieves three things:

1. **Resensitizes receptors** — especially HGW/Maca and Ashwagandha, where continued daily use diminishes effect over time
2. **Reduces hepatic (liver) processing load** — all supplements are metabolized by the liver; one clear day per week is good long-term practice
3. **Prevents psychological dependency** on supplement routines

**The one exception is creatine** — taken in Sunday morning oatmeal to maintain muscle saturation. Missing even one day does not significantly deplete stores, but consistency is the better habit.

### Daily Absorption Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Probiotic always first on empty stomach | Stomach acid in a fed state destroys live cultures before they reach the intestine |
| Never take Zinc and Magnesium together | They compete for the same intestinal transporter — one will be significantly under-absorbed |
| Always take Multivitamin and Fish Oil with a fat-containing meal | Fat-soluble vitamins and omega-3s have near-zero absorption without dietary fat present |
| Magnesium and Ashwagandha together at bedtime | Synergistic — both reduce cortisol and deepen NREM sleep, amplifying each other's effect |
| Creatine with oatmeal (carbohydrate-based meal) | Insulin from carbs drives creatine uptake into muscle cells more efficiently |
| HGW/Maca with water, 30–45 min before training — not with a heavy meal | A full stomach slows gastric emptying and delays the acute pre-workout effect |

## SECTION 7 — Progressive Overload Strategy & 6-Month Milestones

### The Double Progression Method — Applies to Every Exercise

> 1. Start at the **lower end of the rep range** with a weight that is challenging but fully controllable.
> 2. Each session, aim to add **1 rep per set** across all working sets.
> 3. Once you hit the **top of the rep range on all sets** for 2 consecutive sessions, **increase weight by 2.5–5 lbs** on compound lifts or **1.25–2.5 lbs** on isolation exercises, then reset to the bottom of the rep range.
> 4. Never sacrifice form for weight. A slow, controlled rep always beats a sloppy heavy one.

---

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 1–3)

| Parameter | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Compound rep range | 6–10 reps |
| Isolation rep range | 10–20 reps |
| Sets per exercise | 3–4 |
| Progression method | Double progression (reps first, then weight) |
| Rest — compound lifts | 90–120 seconds |
| Rest — isolation lifts | 60–90 seconds |
| Tempo | 2–3 seconds down (eccentric), 1 second up (concentric) |
| Training intensity (RPE) | 7–8 out of 10 — leave 2–3 reps in the tank |
| Cardio | 30–45 min LISS on Thursday active rest only |
| Deload | Week 9 — reduce all weights by 40%, same exercises, prioritize form |

**Key Phase 1 principles:**
- Do not train to failure in the first 8 weeks. Leaving reps in the tank reduces injury risk and improves long-term strength curve.
- Track every session — log the weight and reps for every single set. Progressive overload without tracking is guesswork.
- The slow eccentric (lowering phase) is where the majority of muscle damage and growth stimulus occurs. Never drop the weight.

### Phase 2 — Intensification (Months 4–6)

| Parameter | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Compound rep range | 5–8 reps (heavier loads) |
| Isolation rep range | 10–15 reps |
| Sets per exercise | 4–5 |
| Progression method | Weight progression + advanced techniques |
| Rest — compound lifts | 2–3 minutes |
| Rest — isolation lifts | 60–75 seconds |
| Training intensity (RPE) | 8–9 out of 10 — 1–2 reps left in the tank |
| Cardio | 2× HIIT sessions per week (20 min) + active rest LISS |
| Deload | Week 21 — same protocol as Phase 1 deload |

**Advanced techniques introduced in Phase 2:**

| Technique | Application |
|---|---|
| **Drop Sets** | Final set of lateral raises and curls only — drop weight 30% and do 8–10 more reps immediately |
| **Supersets** | Pair antagonists back-to-back with no rest — e.g., Bicep Curl immediately into Tricep Pushdown |
| **Tempo Work** | 4-1-1 on Bench Press (4 sec down, 1 sec pause at bottom, 1 sec up) — increases time under tension significantly |
| **Rack Pulls** | Replace conventional RDL every other Friday to overload heavier weight and intensify the trap-dominant portion of the pull |

### Critical Non-Negotiables Across All 6 Months

| Priority | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Sleep** | 7–9 hours every night without exception. Muscle is built during sleep, not during training. Magnesium Glycinate 30 min before bed every night it is scheduled. |
| **Protein** | Hit 150g+ of protein every single day. This is the single most important dietary variable. Everything else is secondary. |
| **Consistency** | Missed workouts — not weak workouts — are the enemy of transformation. A bad session is infinitely better than a skipped one. |
| **Hydration** | 2.5–3.5 litres of water daily. Dehydration reduces strength output by up to 15% and significantly impairs recovery. |
| **Stress Management** | Ashwagandha and L-Theanine support this, but manage external stress actively. Chronic high cortisol = fat retention around the stomach and muscle breakdown — the exact opposite of the goal. |
| **Progressive Overload** | Every single session should have one clear goal — one more rep, one more lb, or one cleaner rep than last time. Without progressive overload there is no growth. Period. |
