# CHANGE 1 — RMNP LAKE ADDED: DREAM LAKE (Tuesday Sunrise)

**This is the lake you're looking for.** Dream Lake is one of the most photographed sunrise reflections in Rocky Mountain National Park — Hallett Peak and Flattop Mountain reflect in the still water, and it glows at first light. It's a genuine short hike: 1.1 miles each way, ~425 ft gain, about 45 minutes up from the Bear Lake Trailhead.

**The permit trick (same logic as Maroon Bells):** The Bear Lake Road corridor requires a timed-entry reservation — *but only after 5:00 AM.* If you enter Bear Lake Road before 5 AM, no reservation is needed. Sunrise is 5:31 AM, so you're entering at ~4:10 AM anyway. You slide in under the window.

This means Dream Lake becomes your Tuesday sunrise, and Many Parks Curve shifts to a mid-morning stop on the way up Trail Ridge Road.

**Dream Lake photography:**
At 5:31 AM, first light hits the upper face of Hallett Peak while the lake sits in cool shadow — the peak reflects in the still surface before the wind picks up. The reflection window closes fast (usually calm before 7 AM).
- **16–24mm** — full reflection, Hallett Peak + Flattop Mountain, foreground rocks at water's edge
- **70–200mm** — isolate the peak with its reflection for a tighter symmetrical frame
- Tripod, ISO 100–400, f/8, 1/15–1 sec depending on light
- **Bonus:** Emerald Lake is 0.7 mi further up the same trail — if you have energy, continue past Dream for a second alpine lake.

# CHANGE 2 — CRESTED BUTTE ADDED (Wednesday Sunset)

I'll make this change, but you need two honest facts before committing:

### ⚠️ The June Timing Reality
Crested Butte is the "Wildflower Capital of Colorado" — but **peak bloom is mid-July, not mid-June.** The famous Wildflower Festival runs the second week of July for a reason. On June 17, you'll find **early-season blooms at lower elevations** (lupine, larkspur, early aster along Brush Creek and lower Gothic Road) — not the dense carpets Crested Butte is famous for. The green meadows against the peaks at golden hour are still beautiful, but manage expectations: this is early-season, not peak.

### ⚠️ The Trade-Off
Crested Butte sits 28 miles north of Gunnison. A Crested Butte **sunset** and an **Ouray night** can't both happen Wednesday — they're 2.5 hours apart on the same evening. Adding Crested Butte sunset costs you:

| Lost | Gained |
|---|---|
| Black Canyon Chasm View sunset (8/10) | Crested Butte wildflower sunset |
| Ouray dedicated sunrise (7/10) | Green alpine meadows + Elk Mtns golden hour |
| Ouray Hot Springs (Wed night) | Kebler Pass / Gothic Rd scenery |

You keep Black Canyon as a midday National Park stop (Gunnison Point + Painted Wall) and you still pass through Ouray Thursday morning on the way to Blue Lakes.
